How it works

Record. Walk away.
Notes appear.

Three steps from audio to structured intelligence. The last two happen automatically.

01
Record on your iPhone
Use the built-in Voice Memos app — no new habits required. Record your meeting, call, or brainstorm session as you normally would.
iPhone Voice Memos
02
iCloud syncs it automatically
The moment the recording finishes, iCloud syncs it to your Mac. Phonos detects the new file within seconds and begins processing — no uploading, no drag and drop.
iCloud Watch Auto-detect
03
Structured notes, ready to use
Whisper transcribes the audio with timestamp accuracy. The AI generates structured notes in your chosen format — allocator meeting minutes, client call summary, or general notes. Output is ready for your IC pack, email, or Apple Notes.
